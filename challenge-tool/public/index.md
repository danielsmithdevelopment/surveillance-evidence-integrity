# Challenge the Footage

Surveillance cameras are being used against people in court — and the footage can't always be trusted. Here's how to fight back.

Record a verifiable evidence chain, then generate **FRE 901**, **FRE 702 / Daubert**, **Fourth Amendment**, and **§ 1983** document templates for attorney review. Installable Progressive Web App.
